Client Growth Playbook

The exact Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for our partnership. Ito ang blueprint para tuloy-tuloy ang pasok ng inquiries at benta mo.

Our Core System: KAYO ➔ KAMI ➔ KAYO

Growth is a partnership. [KAYO] You provide the raw business value. [KAMI] We build the Trust & Proof engine so that getting seen actually turns into getting contacted. [KAYO] You close the deals and deliver the service.

Phase 1

The Setup (Foundation)

We Forge Web (KAMI)

  • Audit your current digital footprint and find the "gap" where you're losing clients.
  • Design your High-Converting Landing Page.
  • Optimize your Google Business Profile & Facebook Page.

Your Team (KAYO)

  • Provide accurate service areas and business details.
  • Send us high-quality photos of your past projects/clinic/work.
  • Approve the designs so we can launch.
Phase 2

Traffic & Trust (Launch)

We Forge Web (KAMI)

  • Launch targeted campaigns to bring local eyes to your business.
  • Ensure the Landing Page builds instant trust and positions you as the premium choice.
  • Track where every inquiry comes from.

Your Team (KAYO)

  • Make sure your phone lines and inboxes are monitored daily.
  • Don't keep leads waiting—speed is critical to closing.
Phase 3

Closing & Scaling (Partnership)

We Forge Web (KAMI)

  • Monitor performance and tweak the strategy to lower cost-per-inquiry.
  • Provide reports so you know exactly what's working.
  • Hindi ka namin iiwan sa ere. We maintain the system so it runs 24/7.

Your Team (KAYO)

  • Provide excellent service to your new clients.
  • Ask closed clients to leave reviews on your Google Business Profile (this fuels the system).
  • Give us feedback on lead quality so we can adjust our targeting.
